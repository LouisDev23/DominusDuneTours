SANDWICH HARBOUR SUNSET ADVENTURE

Join Dominus Dune Tours at 4 PM from Swakopmund, Langstrand, or Walvis Bay for an unforgettable adventure where desert dreams meet ocean magic. See pink lakes, flamingos, seals, and jackals, explore Sandwich Harbour’s towering dunes, and finish with a breathtaking sunset over the Atlantic.

Book via WhatsApp

Itinerary

  • 4:00 PM: Departure from Swakopmund, Langstrand, or Walvis Bay.
  • En Route: Visit the surreal pink lakes and shimmering pink sands. Observe wildlife such as flamingos, seals, and jackals.
  • Arrival at Sandwich Harbour: Explore the unique spot where towering dunes meet the Atlantic Ocean, a protected Ramsar site.
  • Dune Drive: Enjoy a thrilling drive through golden dunes.
  • Sunset Viewing: Climb to the crest of a dune to witness the sun setting over the ocean, painting the sky in fiery hues.
  • Return: Head back to your pickup location, concluding the adventure.
