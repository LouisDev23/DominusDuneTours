About the Experience

Ever dreamed of conquering the dunes yourself? With our Rent a Guide experience, you’re not just a passenger—you’re the driver. Your guide isn’t just there to point the way—they’re your co-pilot, sharing fascinating stories about the area’s history, wildlife, and secrets of the desert. Master the timing of the tides as you dash through the extraordinary stretch where the Atlantic Ocean collides with towering dunes—a rare natural phenomenon.

This is the ultimate 4x4 adventure: you’ll learn how to read the sand, navigate slipfaces, and feel the thrill of your vehicle sliding and climbing across the dunes. Every crest you conquer and every stretch of beach you cross is powered by your own hands on the wheel. By the end of the journey, you won’t just have seen the majestic views of Sandwich Harbour—you’ll have earned them.
