SPITZKOPPE GUIDED FULL DAY TOUR

Come and experience the mighty Spitzkoppe towering over the surrounding landscapes. Nicknamed the Matterhorn of Africa because of its sharp peak, Spitzkoppe also hides a lot of history. As you ascend the mountain, a local guide will share fascinating history while showing you rock drawings of the old tribes who once called Spitzkoppe their home. Enjoy the stunning views and the freedom that Spitzkoppe offers!

Pricing Information

Price per person: N$3000

Minimum of 2 persons required for the tour.

This price typically includes:

  • Pick up and drop-off at accommodation (Swakopmund) or self-drive to meeting point.
  • Full Day Tour (approx. 7–9 hours on-site plus driving time).
  • Visit Bushman caves & rock art, scenic viewpoints, and lunch/snacks.
  • Professional local guide and photography assistance.

Located approx. 150km North-East of Swakopmund. Travel times vary depending on stops and road conditions.
