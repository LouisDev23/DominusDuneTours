SANDWICH HARBOUR SELF‑DRIVE – FULL DAY TOUR

Like to do off‑roading? Then this tour is for you. Climb into the seat of your own 4×4 or hired 4×4 and come and experience Sandwich Harbour in all its glory — where you battle the mighty dunes alongside your experienced tour guide. Let’s test your off‑roading skills, shall we?

Pricing Information

Price per car: N$ 3 500

This price includes:

  • Pick‑up and drop‑off at accommodation or self‑drive to meeting point.
  • Guided tour: Test your vehicle & driving skills with our expert guidance.
  • Full day tour (~7 hours).
  • Located approx. 80 km south of Walvis Bay.
  • Off‑road drive along the coast dunes.
  • Spectacular views where the ocean meets the dunes.
  • Wildlife sightings possible (springbuck, oryx, ostrich, seals, seabirds).
  • Food and drinks included.
