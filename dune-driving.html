Master the Art of Dune Driving

Think driving is just about left and right? Think again. Learn to conquer sand driving in the towering dunes of Langstrand, between Swakopmund and Walvis Bay.

Book Your Driving Lesson Today

About the Lessons

Under the guidance of an experienced dune driving instructor, you’ll start with the essentials: deflating your tyres to the perfect pressure for smooth sailing across soft sand. Then the adventure begins. You’ll tackle slipfaces—the steep slopes of the dunes—learning how to approach them with caution and descend with confidence.

You’ll power your way up dune crests, and even take on the thrill of sideways driving, where your instructor will teach you how to stay calm and in control when the vehicle starts to slide.

This isn’t just a driving lesson—it’s an adrenaline-fueled masterclass in desert navigation. You’ll discover how to read the dunes, respect their power, and harness their beauty. By the end, you won’t just know how to drive on sand—you’ll have taken your skills to new heights, literally. From the road to the dunes, this course transforms drivers into adventurers.
