SOSSUSVLEI 2‑DAY GUIDED ADVENTURE

Discover the iconic dunes of Sossusvlei in the Namib Desert on this 2‑day, 1‑night guided experience. Climb towering sand dunes, explore the desert under vast skies, stay overnight and return with memories for a lifetime.

Tour Details & Pricing

Price per person: N$ 10,000 (minimum 2 people).

  • Pick‑up and drop‑off at accommodation or meeting point.
  • Guided dune tours and desert exploration.
  • Accommodation in desert lodge, meals & refreshments included.
  • Transport to and from Sossusvlei.
  • Wildlife and spectacular desert‑coast views.
